THE MR TEAM

Rooted locally.
Ready nationally.

Seven candidates, each with a plan shaped by the place they call home.

01

VICE PRESIDENT · National ticket

Alan Bluespan III

“The citizen, not the system, should be the protagonist.”

An economist and entrepreneur from Chasmia, Alan brings a reformer’s discipline and a builder’s optimism to the national ticket. His 2064 campaign is centred on constitutional government, economic renewal and restoring confidence that public institutions work for citizens.

Constitutional governmentA more competitive economyCaprica’s place in Columbia
02

AMBROSIA · Candidate for MP

Alistair Foulke-McKeon

“Ambrosia should never have to choose between its way of life and a prosperous future.”

Alistair’s plan backs family farms, clean water and the young people who want to make their future in Ambrosia. He is campaigning for reliable freight links, practical apprenticeships and rural services that respect the distance between communities.

Farm export corridorsClean rural waterRegional apprenticeships
03

CHASMIA · Candidate for MP

Patrick Cutter

“Chasmia built Caprica. Now we will build its next generation of industry.”

A former Prime Minister and experienced business leader, Patrick is focused on advanced manufacturing, shipbuilding and skilled work. He wants Chasmia’s research institutions, energy producers and industrial firms working from one long-term regional growth plan.

Advanced manufacturingResearch partnershipsEnergy security
04

MYRATI · Candidate for MP

JB Stoner

“Prosperity must travel beyond the waterfront, and power must travel back to Myrati.”

JB is campaigning for local autonomy, better connections and a tourism economy whose success reaches every community. His programme would keep more visitor revenue on the islands while improving transport, skills and opportunities beyond Port Aaron.

Island transportLocal decision-makingSkills and tourism
05

OPLANA · Candidate for MP

Fernando Henrique Cardoso

“Steady reform can deliver lasting progress.”

Fernando offers experience, calm judgment and a practical agenda for Oplana’s families, coast and growing businesses. His priorities combine coastal resilience with faster primary care, simpler rules for local firms and responsible investment in Langston’s future.

Coastal resilienceSmall businessBetter primary care
06

RENO · Candidate for MP

Roberto Libero

“Keep Reno moving, keep it safe, and keep opportunity within reach.”

Roberto champions the entrepreneurs, commuters and families who make Reno one of Caprica’s most dynamic communities. He is focused on safer neighbourhoods, better links to Montiablo and more housing choices close to jobs, schools and everyday services.

Faster commutesSafer neighbourhoodsHousing choice
07

MONTIABLO · Candidate for MP

Ronan Hayward

“A great capital opens doors, it does not put up barriers.”

MR co-founder and former Mayor-Governor Ronan Hayward has a record of liberal reform and a clear plan for the capital. He is campaigning for homes around transit, open digital government and a city economy that remains active, safe and welcoming after dark.

Homes near transitOpen, digital governmentA thriving night-time economy
